My Personal Favorites

I've traveled to more than fifty countries over a lifetime. These are the places I find myself recommending most — not because they're the most obvious, but because they've stayed with me. Each one offers something totally unique and unforgettable.

Japan

Unlike anywhere on earth. The contrast of ancient ritual and modern precision never gets old.

Italy

The food, the art, the light in Tuscany in the late afternoon. It rewards every visit.

France

Paris is beloved by so many for good reason. But it's Provence and the Loire Valley that keep pulling me back.

Switzerland

The Alps at their most dramatic. Zermatt and Lauterbrunnen belong on every serious traveler's list.

Ireland

The landscape and the people in equal measure. Connemara and the Wild Atlantic Way are extraordinary.

Scotland

Skye and the Highlands are among the most dramatic landscapes I've seen anywhere in the world.

Thailand

A fascinating destination that offers great value for your money. The temples, the food, the warmth of the people.

India — Rajasthan

Overwhelming in the best way. The color, the chaos, the palaces. Nothing prepares you for it.

Norway

The fjords alone would be enough. Add Lofoten and it becomes one of Europe's great journeys.

Vietnam

Hoi An is one of the most beautiful towns in Asia. The whole country surprises you.

New Zealand

The South Island in particular — Queenstown, Fiordland, Milford Sound. Scenery that stops you cold.

Africa — Safari

The Serengeti, Okavango Delta, Namibia. A great safari resets something in you. Everyone should do it at least once.
